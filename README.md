Crooked Rails is a multiplayer game where you work for a suspicious company as a train crew defending your cargo from monsters and trying to complete hauling jobs between stations.

## TUTORIAL
### Controls:
- WASD - move
- Space - jump
- Shift - sprint
- Ctrl - crouch (Not finished)
- E - interact
- R - reload (gun)
- M1 - use (gun/medkit)
- T - show the ammount of money
- Esc - open/close pause menu

### Inventory:
![inventory](https://github.com/AllInTw0/CrookedRailsPrototypeHDRP/blob/main/inventory.png?raw=true)

The way the inventory works is that there are two types of items: tools and non tools. As well as two types of slots: Hand slot (Big one) and "Backpack" slot (Small ones). All non tools go into your "backpack". If you equip a tool it will go into your hand slot, but if your hands are ocupied the tool gets put into your "backpack", usually ocupuing more than one slot.

To interact with your inventory you have these controls:
- Scroll Wheel - cycle through items(The little arrow)
- G - drop
- Q - put in hand slot (Only works with tools)

The little arrow shows which items you are going to drop/swap

### Mini Printer

After Flavortown with this project I participated in an YouShipWeShip called "Stardance" organized by HackClub. Stardance gave me the motivation to finally implement multiplayer and boy was it a big task... It took me almost 60hours to convert the game from singleplayer to multiplayer and the hour count has gone up more, since now when I have to make a feature work both in singleplayer and multiplayer.
